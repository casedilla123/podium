Podium

Practise a talk in front of a virtual audience that reacts to where you look and how you sound. Then see how you did.

Open Podium from your Home Screen for full screen

Safari on iPhone can't hide its address bar, so the two views won't fill the screen or line up with the viewer's lenses.

  1. Tap Safari's Share button (on newer iOS it's inside the ••• menu).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Close Safari and open Podium from the new icon.

Hand-held mode works fine right here in Safari.

Before you put the phone in the viewer
  • Open Podium from its Home Screen icon so there are no browser bars.
  • Turn Portrait Orientation Lock off. Podium needs landscape.
  • Turn Low Power Mode off, brightness up, Do Not Disturb on.
  • Ringer on, volume up: the room has sound.
  • Allow motion and microphone access when asked.
How it works in the viewer
  • Look at a button for about a second to pick it, or press the viewer's button.
  • Press and hold for one second to recenter at any time.
  • Talk to the room. People you look at perk up; people you ignore drift off.
  • Press during a talk to bring up "End talk?".
Desktop debug keys
  • Drag to look, click to tap, R recenter
  • S stereo, G gaze credit, I interest bars, P performance
  • Hold Space to fake speaking, T 4× time
Turn your phone sideways and put it in the viewer.
