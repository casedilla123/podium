Podium

Give a talk to a virtual audience that reacts to where you look. Then see how you did.

Saved on this phone only. They show on the podium during your talk.

Before you put the phone in the viewer
  • Open Podium from its Home Screen icon so there are no browser bars.
  • Turn Portrait Orientation Lock off. Podium needs landscape.
  • Turn Low Power Mode off. It can throttle animation.
  • Brightness up, Do Not Disturb on.
  • Allow motion access when asked.
How it works in the viewer
  • Look at a button for about a second to select it, or press the viewer's button.
  • Press and hold for one second to recenter at any time.
  • Press during a talk to bring up "End talk?".
Desktop debug keys
  • Drag to look, click to tap, R recenter
  • S stereo, G gaze credit, I interest bars, P performance
  • Hold Space to fake speaking, T 4× time
Turn your phone sideways and put it in the viewer.
